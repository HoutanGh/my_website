Support

Kanhwa

Questions, problems or ideas: email houtangh.apps@gmail.com. I read every message.

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you did and what happened;
  • if the app shows an error, its details: tap Copy error details and paste them into your email.

Adding something to read

  • A link: tap + in the Library, paste a comic or chapter link, and tap Open link.
  • From Safari: open the page, tap Share, then Read in app.
  • Your own pictures: tap +, then Photos or Files, and pick the pages of one chapter.

Some websites cannot be read automatically. If a link does not work, open the page in Safari until the chapter or chapter list is visible, then use Share → Read in app.

Translation

Translation runs on your iPhone using Apple's built-in technology. It supports Korean → English, Spanish → English and Korean → Spanish. The first time you use a language, iOS may ask to download it. Translation works best on clear digital pages and screenshots; photos of printed pages may translate less well.

You can translate up to five new chapters each week.

Your content

Kanhwa does not host, distribute or provide comics. Translate only content you have the right to access.
