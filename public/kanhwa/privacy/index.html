Privacy Policy

Kanhwa · Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect any personal data. What you read and translate stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who makes the app

Kanhwa is made by an independent developer (HoutanGh). You can reach me at houtangh.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything the app saves is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app:

  • the chapters and pictures you add, and their translations;
  • your reading position and reading history;
  • your settings, such as translation languages;
  • the count of free chapters used this week.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. Saved chapter pages are kept out of iCloud and computer backups, so they stay only on this iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Websites you open. When you paste a link or open a website in the app, the app connects directly to that website to load its pages and images, as a web browser would. That website receives what any website receives from a browser, such as your IP address and the pages requested, and may store cookies for its own use. The website's own privacy policy applies to that. When the app downloads pages itself, it tells the website it is this app, using its name and version.
  • Safari's Read in app. When you choose Share → Read in app in Safari, the page you are viewing is passed to the app on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to me.
  • Translation. Text recognition and translation run on your iPhone using Apple's built-in technology. iOS may download translation languages from Apple the first time you use them; Apple's privacy policy covers that.
  • Crash reports. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports about the app with me. They do not identify you.

Photos and files

When you add your own pictures, iOS shows its own photo or file picker, and the app receives only the pictures you choose. The app copies them into its storage; your originals in Photos or Files are not changed. The app cannot see the rest of your photo library or files.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code from other companies.
  • It does not sell or share your information, because it does not collect any.

Deleting your data

You can delete any chapter in the app: long-press it in the Library and choose Delete from library. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Your own pictures in Photos or Files are not affected.

Children

The app is rated 16+ in the App Store and is not intended for children.

Your rights

Because I do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about privacy, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
